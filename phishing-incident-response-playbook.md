# Phishing Incident Response Playbook

## Project Type

Beginner SOC Analyst Practice Project

## Disclaimer

This is a simulated cybersecurity project created for educational and portfolio purposes.

---

# Purpose

This playbook provides a simple step-by-step process for responding to a suspected phishing email.

The goal is to help security analysts investigate the email, reduce possible risks, and take appropriate action.

---

# When to Use This Playbook

This playbook can be used when:

- An employee reports a suspicious email.
- An email requests sensitive information.
- An email contains a suspicious link.
- An email contains a suspicious attachment.
- An employee may have entered their credentials.

---

# Step 1: Identify the Alert

Collect basic information about the suspicious email:

- Sender's email address
- Recipient's email address
- Email subject
- Date and time received
- Links or attachments

---

# Step 2: Analyze the Email

Check for phishing indicators such as:

- Urgent or threatening language
- Requests for passwords or sensitive information
- Suspicious sender addresses
- Misspelled domains
- Suspicious links
- Unexpected attachments

---

# Step 3: Assess the Risk

Determine whether:

- The sender is legitimate.
- The link appears suspicious.
- The attachment may be harmful.
- Other employees received the same email.
- The employee clicked a link.
- The employee entered their credentials.

---

# Step 4: Containment

If the email is confirmed or strongly suspected to be phishing:

- Prevent further interaction with the email.
- Block or investigate the sender.
- Remove similar phishing emails if necessary.
- Warn affected users.

---

# Step 5: If Credentials Were Entered

If an employee entered their username or password:

- Reset the password.
- Review account activity.
- Check for suspicious logins.
- Enable or verify Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Monitor the account for suspicious activity.

---

# Step 6: Document the Incident

Record:

- What happened
- Who was affected
- Indicators of phishing
- Actions taken
- Investigation findings
- Recommendations

---

# Step 7: Lessons Learned

After the incident:

- Identify how the phishing attempt reached the user.
- Review whether security controls worked.
- Consider additional employee awareness training.
- Improve the playbook if necessary.

---

# Conclusion

This playbook provides a structured process for responding to suspected phishing incidents.

Following a documented process can help security teams respond consistently and reduce the chance of missing important investigation steps.
