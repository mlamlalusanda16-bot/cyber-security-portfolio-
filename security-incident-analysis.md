# Security Incident Analysis

## About

This section documents my learning about cybersecurity incidents and how security professionals investigate and respond to them.

## What I Have Learned

I have learned that a security incident can affect an organization's systems, data, employees, and business operations.

A cybersecurity team needs to investigate incidents to understand what happened and reduce further damage.

## Incident Investigation

During an investigation, security professionals may need to:

- Identify what happened
- Determine which systems are affected
- Analyze security logs
- Identify suspicious activity
- Investigate the possible cause
- Assess the impact on the organization

## Business Impact

Cybersecurity incidents can affect normal business operations.

For example, if an important server stops working, employees may be unable to provide services to customers.

This can cause:

- Service disruptions
- Loss of productivity
- Financial losses
- Customer dissatisfaction

## Response

A cybersecurity team may need to:

1. Identify the incident
2. Investigate the cause
3. Contain the problem
4. Reduce further damage
5. Restore affected systems
6. Document what happened
