# Phishing Email Investigation

## Project Type

Beginner Cybersecurity Practice Scenario

## Scenario

An employee receives the following email:

**From:** security-update@company-support.com  
**Subject:** Urgent: Your Account Will Be Suspended

The email says:

> Your account will be suspended unless you verify your password immediately. Click the link below to verify your account.

The email contains a link asking the employee to enter their username and password.

---

## Initial Observation

Several signs indicate that this email may be suspicious:

- It creates a sense of urgency.
- It threatens account suspension.
- It asks the user to click a link.
- It requests sensitive information.
- The sender's address may not be an official company address.

---

## Possible Threat

This email may be a **phishing attack**.

The attacker may be trying to trick the employee into providing their username and password.

---

## Investigation Steps

I would investigate:

1. The sender's email address.
2. Whether the domain is legitimate.
3. The destination of the link.
4. Whether other employees received the same email.
5. Whether the email contains suspicious attachments.
6. Whether anyone entered their credentials.

---

## Potential Impact

If an employee enters their credentials, an attacker could potentially:

- Access the employee's account.
- Access sensitive information.
- Send more phishing emails.
- Gain unauthorized access to company systems.

---

## Recommended Actions

- Do not click the suspicious link.
- Report the email to the security team.
- Block or investigate the sender.
- Warn other employees.
- Reset passwords if credentials were entered.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

---

## Conclusion

Based on the available information, I would classify this email as a **suspected phishing attempt**.

Further investigation would be required to confirm whether the sender and link are malicious.
